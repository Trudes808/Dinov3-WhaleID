PIPELINE
  lm_feats.py   ALIKED-n16 (kornia, 1024-px copy of the 1536-px parts_up chart from the RAW), keypoints only on the whale
                mask dilated 6 px (outline kept), top 1024 by score; fins also on the mirrored chart. Up to 3 instances per
                cluster: largest part (pixels in the RAW crop) + 0.5 x log Laplacian sharpness. --fallback: flukes of clusters
                with no cut-out get a square box crop of the 2560-px copy + canon mask (val only in the end).
  lm_match.py   LightGlue (aliked weights), batched with padding masks (checked identical to single-pair calls after fixing
                the softmax over padded columns), filter 0.1; RANSAC on CPU: similarity (scale 0.5-2, rotation <= 30 deg),
                affine, homography (MAGSAC), thresholds 0.6 % / 1.2 % of the chart. Val days 3 x 3 instance pairs (+ mirror for
                fins); train days 2 x 2, no mirror (GPU queue). Cluster pair = max over instance pairs; all bench numbers use
                the first 2 picks per cluster on every day.
  lm_geo.py     offline geometry from stored matches: interior-only matches (>= 15 px inside the mask), inlier 2-D spread.
  lm_dense.py   EfficientLoFTR (HF zju-community/efficientloftr) at 640 px, matches kept on whale pixels, same RANSAC.
  lm_score.py / lm_screen.py  scores -> bench; lm_fuse2.py  Platt(BASE) + positive isotonic local evidence (train-fitted);
  lm_fuse.py    z-mean and logistic fusions; lm_viz.py top pairs (images in the scratchpad only); lm_marks.py researcher marks.

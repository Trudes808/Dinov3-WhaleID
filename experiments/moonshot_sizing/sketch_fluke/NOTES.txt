sketch_fluke (2026-11-18): automatic researcher-style fluke drawings (contour + light / dark pattern) and their comparison
on the moonshot bench. CPU only, no eval day read, nothing fitted except the fusion / veto (16 pool days of each bench
split). Arrays in moonshot_cache/sketch_fluke, sheets in the scratchpad (sketch_fluke/), none in the repo.

PIPELINE
  prep.py    parts_up fluke cut-outs at 384 px, flipped blades-up, masks, the aligned agent's tip / notch points mapped in
  draw.py    canonical canvas 256 x 128 from the two tips (similarity); seen = whale mask eroded 2 px, blades only;
             brightness n = (L - p5 on the whale) / (p99.5 of the cut-out - p5); drawing = dark (n < .35) / grey / light (> .5)
             + outline; per cluster a consensus (median n over the frames, kept where >= half the frames saw it,
             'stable' = share of frames agreeing on light / dark)
  build.py   cache of every instance's drawing + template (mean silhouette of clear light underside views)
  score.py   drawable gate: tips from the point model and on the mask, span >= 250 RAW px, not cut by the frame, blade
             area >= .045 span^2, >= 60 % of the seen mask inside the loose template and >= 30 % of it seen;
             comparison at 128 x 64: best Pearson r of the blurred n over the common seen region (15 small shifts);
             light-share difference, contradiction share (clear light vs clear dark), 5-step light class
  run.py     1 bench.evaluate_pairs (sketch_fluke_any, sketch_fluke_light) | 2 exclusion | 3 class repeatability |
             4 fusion + veto on the 20 splits (logs run1.log, run23.log, run4.log; exclusion.json, classes.json, fusion.json)
  marks.py   researcher's marked regions (2025-01-02) against the drawn marks (marks.log, marks.json)
  sheet.py   sheets

COUNTS  3444 fluke instances on the 24 days; 2703 with a cut-out; 802 drawable (23 % of instances); 476 of 1279 clusters
  have a drawing (223 from >= 2 frames); 67 of them are light enough (>= 15 % light blade) to be called underside views.
  Pairs drawn: 1641 of 16617 (9.9 %); both light: 31.

TOP / BOTTOM  no usable label. The notch-vs-tip-line geometry in the upright photo and the parts.py CSE 'underside' flag

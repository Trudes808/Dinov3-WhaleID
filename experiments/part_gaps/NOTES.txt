Files
  sample.json              609 real whale instances: 401 from the 21 training days, 208 from the 3 validation days,
                           stratified by the view classifier's confident three-way answer (or "uncertain") x box size;
                           weight = stratum size / sampled (population estimates). Not-whale instances excluded.
  labels_ai.txt            AI labels (Claude subagent), one category + attributes per instance, typed from BLIND sheets
                           (photo crop + instance outline, no model output). NOT the researcher's labels.
  labels_ai_relook.txt     second looks, also blind: (1) every B_back / H_head re-asked "is a fin, or its end-on spike at
                           the apex of an arched back, in view?" (16 changed); (2) the 58 instances whose label disagreed
                           with the view classifier, adjudicated on larger tiles (5 changed, 17 marked ambiguous a / a2).
  report_<model>_<variant>.json   the gap table and every breakdown (gap_table = the ranked rows).
  nearest_<model>_<variant>.json  nearest renders (frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16 whale tokens; silhouette IoU) per instance.
  res_test_<model>.json    inference-resolution test (per-instance window at output caps 384 / 768 (live) / 1024).

Taxonomy (common.CATS): F_up, F_low (rising low / rolling out), F_edge (side-on / single blade), F_tips; D_tall,
D_small (small / stubby / low fin, often a long low back), D_tip (end-on spike from behind, far or partly submerged);
B_back (no fin in view), P_pec, H_head, R_roll, X_none. Attributes s1 / s2 splash, m other whale, e photo edge,
b blur, h hump, k hooked, w water sheeting, a / a2 still ambiguous after the second look.

Rule: every model's fluke / fin / other call uses its own real-photo outputs and the rule re-chosen on the training days in
evaluate_real's two-part family (as experiments/realwater/compare.py). Cost = share x error. Ranking on the training-day
sample; validation-day numbers are the reported ones. Cause column: "view missing" = coverage in the training renders
(run05) < 0.5; "model / label rule / resolution" = the model fails >= 25 % on >= 15 renders of the category;
"appearance / shape" = covered and fine on renders, wrong on real; "minor" = real error < 12 %.
